# Ethics, Consent, Licensing, and AI Disclosure

This document defines the ethical guardrails, AI transparency rules, model/output licensing records, and consent tracking for **The Monument That Lives in My Memory**.

---

## 1. Core Ethical Boundaries

1. **Clear AI Disclosure:**
   - All AI-generated visuals, audio, and music must be transparently disclosed.
   - The finished film will include a clear **AI disclosure end card**.
   - Any social or public posting (e.g., LinkedIn, YouTube) will enable the platform's AI-generated content label where available and state in the post that AI tools were used.
   - Embedded provenance metadata or watermarks (such as SynthID or C2PA, where generated by providers) will be preserved rather than stripped.

2. **No Misrepresentation as Historical Evidence:**
   - AI-generated reconstructions of Harar or the Ras Makonnen Monument represent **personal memory and artistic reconstruction**, never authentic historical photographs, archival footage, or documentary proof.

3. **No Invented Historical Facts:**
   - Do not fabricate dates, historical claims, architectural specifics, or statistics about Harar or the monument. Stick strictly to verified personal experience and established context.

4. **Cultural and Personal Respect:**
   - Treat Harar's heritage, community spaces, and personal family memories with dignity and care.
   - Avoid feeding sensitive private family photos or identifying personal details into external free endpoints that may train on submitted inputs.

5. **Non-Political Storytelling:**
   - Keep the narrative strictly personal, reflective, and emotional.
   - Do not introduce political commentary, criticism, blame, or partisan framing regarding the monument's demolition.

6. **Consent for Likeness and Voice:**
   - No real person's face, recognizable likeness, or cloned/recorded voice may be generated or included without their explicit, recorded informed consent covering both the use and the words spoken.
