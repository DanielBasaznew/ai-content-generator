# Audience and Film Intention

### Project Title
**The Monument That Lives in My Memory**

---

### 1. Target Audience

The film is designed for three connected audiences:

- **People from Harar and Ethiopia:** Viewers who may recognize the cultural and emotional significance of familiar places and landmarks.
- **People with similar personal experiences:** Anyone who has lost a place associated with childhood, family experiences, or important memories.
- **International audiences:** Viewers unfamiliar with Harar who can still connect with universal themes of childhood, nostalgia, belonging, and cultural heritage.

The storytelling will provide enough context for international viewers to understand the personal significance of the monument without requiring prior knowledge of Harar.

---

### 2. Film Intention

The intention is to tell a personal story about the emotional connection between people and places.

Through my childhood memories of the Ras Makonnen Monument in Harar, the film explores how an ordinary landmark can become part of someone's identity and personal history.

The film aims to help viewers reflect on their own meaningful places and understand that heritage is not limited to physical structures. It also exists in the memories and emotions people carry.

---

### 3. Intended Emotional Journey

The film will take viewers through a gradual emotional progression:

1. **Happiness:** Introduce childhood memories and the excitement associated with passing the monument.
2. **Nostalgia:** Reflect on growing up, leaving Harar, and spending years away.
3. **Sadness and loss:** Reveal the absence of the monument upon returning home.
4. **Reflection:** Allow viewers to consider the relationship between physical places, personal memories, and identity.

The emotional contrast between the joyful childhood memories and the disappointing return is central to the storytelling.

---

### 4. Central Message

**Physical landmarks may disappear, but the memories and emotions connected to them remain.**

Supporting ideas:
- Places can become part of our personal identity.
- Cultural heritage includes personal and collective memories.
- Meaningful places deserve appreciation and preservation.

---

### 5. Storytelling Approach

The film will use **first-person personal storytelling**.

The narration will be told from my perspective, using my own childhood experiences and memories rather than presenting the story as an external historical documentary.

The visual storytelling will support the narration through cinematic scenes, atmospheric imagery, and AI-generated visual reconstructions where appropriate.

The film will distinguish creative AI reconstructions from authentic historical photographs or documentary evidence.

---

### 6. Intended Ending

The film will end on an emotional and reflective note, emphasizing the sadness of returning to a familiar place and discovering that an important part of one's childhood environment is gone.

The ending will not introduce political criticism or assign blame. It will focus on personal loss, memory, and the emotional meaning of home.

---

### 7. LinkedIn Publishing Objective

The film has three publishing objectives:

1. **Personal storytelling:** Share a meaningful experience from my childhood and the heritage of my hometown, Harar.
2. **Technical demonstration:** Demonstrate my ability to use AI tools and creative technologies to produce a complete short film.
3. **Creative exploration:** Show how AI can support storytelling and help communicate personal and cultural experiences.

The final publication should communicate both the emotional story and the creative process behind its production.

---

### 8. Creative Boundaries

- Keep the story personal and non-political.
- Do not introduce political criticism, blame, or unsupported claims.
- Do not invent historical facts about the monument.
- Do not present AI-generated reconstructions as authentic historical photographs.
- Preserve the emotional authenticity of my actual experience.
- Ensure that international viewers can understand the story without extensive historical background.

---
